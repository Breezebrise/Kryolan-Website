Very pale skin

Too light for every range? Mix your own

Very pale skin runs into the opposite problem from olive. The lightest shade in a range is often still too dark, or it oxidizes and turns orange by midday. The fix pros use is a white mixer. Add a drop to any foundation to lift it a shade or two without changing the undertone much.

Kryolan already makes them. Supracolor 070 white, TV Paint Stick TV white and Ultra Foundation white are pure white bases. Mix a tiny amount into your shade with a spatula, then check the jawline. Too much white looks chalky, so add less than you think.

Liquid mixers work with liquid foundations: NYX Pro Foundation Mixer, L.A. Girl Pro Color Foundation Mixing Pigment in White, and Manic Panic Dreamtone in Virgin Whitest White.

Fixing the undertone: if your palest shade looks pink, a touch of Kryolan yellow veil corrects it. If it looks yellow or orange, cut it with a cooler pale shade like NB or alabaster.

Colors on pale skin: pale skin shows the difference between reds instantly. Use the red test above. Cool-leaning pale skin usually wants berry, raspberry or blue-red. Warm-leaning pale skin wants coral or a clear orange-red.

Seasonal color analysis is a guide, not a rule. It is usually done in person with your real hair and eyes. Shade data comes from each brand's own shade names and descriptions, October 2026. Always swatch on your jawline in daylight.
